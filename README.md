## About

GhostGuessr is a utility addon for GeoGuessr in the browser and on Steam.

## Features

- **Ghost marker**: shows the round's start location on the guess map (hotkey `1` or the round HUD button)
- **Street View marker**: follows your current position while you move
- **Modes** (choose one): **Normal** shows the exact spot. **Safety** only outlines the country (or state) the round is in, using bundled Natural Earth borders, so it needs no network. **Rage** places your guess on the exact spot for you, either automatically when the round starts (**Auto pin**) or when you press the pin hotkey (default `2`)
- **Score predictor**: estimated points and distance for your current guess, plus an optional connection line
- **Streamer mode** (web): blurs names, avatars, flags and club tags
- **Auto-claim rewards** (web): daily shop and level-up rewards
- **Skinchanger** (web + Steam): try every avatar item locally; toggling and saving apply live without a page reload

All settings live in a **GhostGuessr tab** in GeoGuessr's own settings dialog (next to *General* and
*Keybindings*) and in a **GhostGuessr** entry of the main-menu settings page (Steam home and
account settings). The GhostGuessr hotkeys are also listed in the *Keybindings* tab, and the round
ghost-marker button sits above the ⚙ button in the in-game HUD. There are no extra windows.
The markup reference is in `new-style-website.txt`.
